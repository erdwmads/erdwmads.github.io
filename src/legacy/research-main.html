Research focus

What controlled dolomite formation in Orgueil?

My graduation research studies dolomite in the Orgueil CI1 chondrite. I connect mineral textures, chemistry and crystal structure to investigate aqueous alteration and material evolution within a primitive parent body.

Orgueil is the research target. Returned samples from Ryugu and Bennu provide independent comparisons for the mineral record of water; they do not establish Orgueil's parent body.
