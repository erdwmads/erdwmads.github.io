02 / The analytical pathway

From observation to interpretation

The project connects texture, chemistry and crystal structure. Each method contributes a different part of the mineral record.

  1. 01

    Sample selection

    Identify suitable Orgueil CI1 material and carbonate-bearing regions.

  2. 02

    SEM / BSE

    Document morphology, texture and spatial relations to clay-rich areas.

  3. 03

    EPMA mapping

    Constrain elemental distribution and carbonate chemistry.

  4. 04

    EBSD-guided FIB preparation

    EBSD mapping will be used to select and orient regions for focused ion beam (FIB) sections and a single grain.

  5. 05

    Single-grain XRD

    A FIB-prepared grain will be used to test crystal structure and carbonate ordering.

  6. 06

    TEM / EDS / diffraction

    FIB sections will be examined for nanoscale interfaces, defects and local structure.

  7. 07

    Formation conditions

    Integrate the observations to discuss formation conditions during parent-body aqueous alteration.

The analytical pathway guides the investigation; it is not a checklist of completed results. Read the methodological references →
