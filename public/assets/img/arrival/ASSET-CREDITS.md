# Earth imagery

Downloaded 2026-09-09 from the official Three.js example texture collection:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets

- earth-day.jpg: earth_day_4096.jpg
- earth-night.jpg: earth_night_4096.jpg
- earth-clouds.png: earth_clouds_1024.png
- earth-day-2048.webp, earth-day-1024.webp, earth-night-2048.webp, earth-night-1024.webp: WebP derivatives (sharp, lanczos3, quality 82) of earth-day.jpg and earth-night.jpg, used by the entrance and the orbital view. earth-day.jpg (4096) remains for the sample-missions Earth close-up (src/scripts/sample-missions/scenes.js); earth-night.jpg is kept only as the source of its WebP derivatives.

The repository's MIT license is retained in THREE-LICENSE.txt.
The maps are static imagery, not live weather or live city-light observations.

The galaxy and Solar System are artistic scientific illustrations. Sizes, separations, phases and speeds are compressed for the entrance sequence, not measured scale or current ephemerides. No NASA affiliation or endorsement is implied.
